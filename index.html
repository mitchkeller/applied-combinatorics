Applied Combinatorics

This is a text with more than enough material for a one-semester introduction to combinatorics. The original target audience was primarily computer science majors, but the topics included make it suitable for a variety of different students. Topics include basic enumeration, recursion and mathematical induction, graph theory, partially ordered sets, additional enumeration techniques, and graph algorithms.
